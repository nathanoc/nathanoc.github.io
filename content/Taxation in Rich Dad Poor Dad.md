According to [[Rich Dad Poor Dad]], the rich do not allow themselves to be taxed, while the poor and middle class do because they do not understand money.

Some objections:
- Tax loopholes only work for those rich enough to afford them
- In principle, the rich should be taxed more (both because they don't need the wealth, and to prevent runaway inequality)
