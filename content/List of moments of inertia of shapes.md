[[Moment of inertia of a disc]]: $\frac{1}{2}mr^2$
[[Moment of inertia of a rod about an arbitrary point on the rod's axis]]: $a^2 m + amL + \frac{1}{3} m L^2 - \frac{ma^3}{3L}$ where $a$ is the distance of the arbitrary point from the closer end of the rod
