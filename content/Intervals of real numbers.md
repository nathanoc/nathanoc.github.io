The closed interval $[u..v]$ is the set of real numbers $x$ such that $u \leq x \leq v$.
The open interval $(u..v)$ is the set of real numbers $x$ such that $u < x < v$. At an open endpoint, $u$ can be $-\infty$ or $v$ can be $\infty$.
Half-open intervals are written as $[u..v)$ or $(u..v]$.
