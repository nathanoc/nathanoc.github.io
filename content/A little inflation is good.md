Inflation means that if people hold on to their money it disappears. This encourages them to convert their money to tangible things - i.e., to spend and invest. This boosts economic growth. 

With deflation, people are incentivised not to spend at all, which can lead to a "deflationary spiral" where lower demand causes prices to keep falling. A deflationary spiral can be almost impossible to escape.
